Methodology & Sources

Every number in our calculator is traceable. This page explains the formula and cites the exact public data we used — so you (and search engines) can verify it.

⚠️
Estimates for planning only — never a binding quote. Figures are built from published 2026 market benchmarks and climate data, then run through a transparent formula. Your real price depends on your exact lawn, access, and local competition. Always confirm with a local provider before you buy.

1. The formula

The calculator applies a small set of transparent multipliers to a base mowing rate, then adds any selected services.

Per-visit mowing price

mowPerVisit = max(area × baseRate, baseMin) × condition × (1 + terrain) × (1 + obstacles) × (1 − planDiscount) × stateIndex

  • baseRate / baseMin — base mowing price per sq ft (floor applied for small lawns).
  • condition — grass height/state: short 0.9 · normal 1.0 · tall 1.45 · overgrown 1.9.
  • terrain — flat +0% · gentle +10% · moderate +20% · steep +35%.
  • obstacles — none +0% · moderate +15% · heavy +30%.
  • planDiscount — weekly −15% · bi-weekly −10% · monthly −5% (recurring saves the crew routing time).
  • stateIndex — your state/province cost-of-living index ÷ 100.

Recurring vs one-time

  • One-time: annual cost = per-visit price (1 visit).
  • Recurring: visits per year = visitsPerMonth × seasonMonths; monthly = per-visit × visitsPerMonth; annual = monthly × seasonMonths. So monthly × season = annual always holds.

Add-on services

serviceCost = max(rate × area, min) × (1 + terrain) × (1 + obstacles) × stateIndex × visitsPerSeason (mulch & yard cleanup use a flat per-visit price instead of a per-sq-ft rate).

5. Limitations & how to verify

  • No official "lawn mowing price" exists. No government publishes a per-state mowing rate. We proxy regional cost with the Cost of Living Index, which is the most defensible public surrogate for local labour/overhead.
  • Season length is an estimate. It reflects the frost-free growing window, not a surveyed "months people mow." Northern states are conservative.
  • Prices are 2026 snapshots. Marketplace ranges shift with fuel, labour, and demand. Re-check the sources each season.
  • Local variation is real. Within a state, urban cores and resort areas run above the index; rural areas below.
  • Best practice: treat the output as a budgeting range, then get 2–3 local quotes. If you operate a lawn business, replace the BASE, REGIONS, and SERVICES values in index.html with your own actual pricing — the formula stays valid.
