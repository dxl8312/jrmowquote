What this policy covers

This page describes the process we actually follow for the calculator methodology, source citations and cost guides on JrMowQuote. It applies to content published on this site and is not a statement of third-party practices.

How we research and verify

  • Calculation methods. The pricing formula is implemented as plain functions in the site's front-end code. We verify it with automated tests and worked examples; the full formula, inputs, bounds and step-by-step examples are published on the Methodology page. Our internal verification record confirms the formula and its tolerance against the test cases.
  • Cost inputs. Where we use published ranges (for example, national service price bands), we name the source and state what it does and does not support. A cost-of-living index is used only as a regional proxy; we do not present it as a surveyed lawn-care price.
  • Estimates and limits. Climate-derived season lengths and regional multipliers are labelled as estimates with their confidence level and known limitations on the Methodology page.
  • Keyword and search data. Where third-party metrics (search volume, KD, CPC) are not available, we mark them as unavailable rather than estimating them. First-party Search Console observations are treated as low-sample signals and are not presented as stable rankings.

How we write and review

  • Content is written to describe what the tools actually do, using the calculator's own outputs so prose and tools cannot drift apart.
  • We check figures against the tool output and against the cited source before publishing. Fact-checking corrections made during preparation are recorded internally.
  • We do not claim review by a licensed professional, industry expert, or editorial board. No such review is represented as having occurred.

How we update and correct

  • Data changes are recorded in a Data Update Log with the date, page, changed data, source and reason. The log starts empty and no entries are backfilled that were not actually made.
  • Material corrections to published figures will be reflected on the affected page and, where applicable, in the update log.
  • We do not present planned work as completed, or unapproved integrations (advertising, affiliate programs) as active.

Independence

The pricing engine is independent of any advertising or affiliate relationship. We do not rank or recommend providers based on commissions. Any commercial relationship will be described on the site only once it is actually in effect.

Authorship and review

No individual author or reviewer is credited on this site at present. This page does not claim expert, professional or editorial-board review, and no reviewer name or credential is represented.
