How the calculation works

depth (ft) = depth (in) / 12 cu ft = area (sq ft) x depth (ft) cu yd = cu ft / 27 bags = ceil(cu ft / bag cu ft) cost = bags x price per bag

1 ft = 12 in and 1 cu yd = 27 cu ft are unit definitions. Bags are rounded up to whole bags. No waste or settling allowance is included.

All results are based only on the parameters you entered. This tool does not recommend a depth, supply bag sizes or prices, or include waste and settling. Follow the product label and your own situation.
