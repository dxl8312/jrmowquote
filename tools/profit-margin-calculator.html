How the calculation works

total cost = sum of your cost items gross profit = revenue - total cost margin % = gross profit / revenue x 100 markup % = gross profit / total cost x 100 (needs cost > 0) required quote = total cost / (1 - target margin / 100)

Blank cost fields are treated as 0 and noted in the result. A target margin of 100% or more is rejected to avoid division by zero.

Results reflect only the revenue and costs you entered. We provide no typical margins, cost benchmarks or pricing advice. Judge the outcome against your own business and local market.
